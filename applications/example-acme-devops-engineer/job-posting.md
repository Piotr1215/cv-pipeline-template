# Acme Cloud: Senior DevOps Engineer

Source: https://example.com/jobs/123
Location: Remote (EU)

## About the role

Acme Cloud runs a multi-tenant SaaS platform on Kubernetes across AWS and GCP.
You will own the delivery pipeline and the platform that 40 engineers deploy to
every day, and you will be the person they come to when a deploy goes wrong.

## What you will do

- Own CI/CD and GitOps for 100+ services, and keep deployments boring.
- Run and evolve the Kubernetes platform: upgrades, autoscaling, cost.
- Build golden paths so product teams self-serve infrastructure safely.
- Carry the pager with the team and turn incidents into fixes.

## What we look for

- 5+ years running production infrastructure on Kubernetes.
- Terraform and GitOps (ArgoCD or Flux) in anger, not in a tutorial.
- A track record of cutting cloud cost without cutting reliability.
- You write things down: runbooks, design notes, postmortems.
